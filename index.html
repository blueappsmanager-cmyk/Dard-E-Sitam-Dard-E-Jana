1Introduction

Welcome to Dard-E-Sitam, Dard-E-Jana ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (com.bluenoveljana.mywebapp).

By downloading and using Dard-E-Sitam, Dard-E-Jana, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2App Features

Dard-E-Sitam, Dard-E-Jana is designed to provide an immersive novel reading experience with the following features:

📖 Real Book Reading Authentic page-turning experience
🔖 Bookmarks & Resume Save progress and resume anytime
🔍 Pinch to Zoom Zoom and drag to move pages
📤 Share Novels Share via WhatsApp, Facebook, Twitter & more

3Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Reading preferences and bookmark data stored locally on your device
  • Any feedback or correspondence you send us

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, OS version, screen resolution)
  • App usage statistics (pages read, time spent reading)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data for app improvement
  • IP address and general location (country/region level only)

Local Storage

Your bookmarks, reading progress, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your reading history or bookmarks to our servers unless you explicitly enable cloud backup.

4How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  1. Provide and maintain the reading experience and app functionality
  2. Save your bookmarks and resume reading position across sessions
  3. Enable zoom and page navigation features
  4. Facilitate sharing novels with friends through third-party apps
  5. Improve app performance and fix technical issues
  6. Understand reading trends to enhance our novel collection
  7. Send important app updates and notifications (if opted in)

5Device Permissions

To provide our features, Dard-E-Sitam, Dard-E-Jana may request the following permissions:

  • Storage: To save bookmarks, reading progress, and cached novel content locally on your device
  • Internet: To download novel content and receive app updates
  • Share/Intent: To enable sharing novels via WhatsApp, Facebook, Twitter, and other social platforms

You can manage these permissions through your device settings at any time.

6Information Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy. We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Analytics Providers: Anonymous usage data shared with trusted analytics services (e.g., Firebase Analytics) to help us improve the app
  • Social Sharing: When you choose to share a novel, the shared content is sent directly through your selected platform (WhatsApp, Facebook, Twitter, etc.)
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights and safety
  • Service Providers: With trusted partners who assist us in operating the app

7Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

  • All data transmissions are encrypted using industry-standard protocols
  • Your reading progress and bookmarks are stored securely on your device
  • Regular security updates to protect against vulnerabilities
  • Limited access to any server-stored data by authorized personnel only

While we strive to protect your data, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

8Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request information about what data we hold about you
  • Delete: Clear your local app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app cache
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics and crash reporting in app settings
  • Control: Manage app permissions through your device settings

9Children's Privacy

Dard-E-Sitam, Dard-E-Jana is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove such data.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated policy within the app and updating the effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
